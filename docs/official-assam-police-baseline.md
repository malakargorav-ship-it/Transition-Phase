## General Constable written test

The 05-12-2025 advertisement (sections 7.8.D–7.8.E) specifies:

- 100 OMR multiple-choice questions.
- 0.5 mark for each correct answer; total 50 marks.
- Class IX–X level.
- No negative marking.
- Subjects: Elementary Arithmetic; General English; Logical Reasoning/Mental Ability; Assam's History, Geography, Polity and Economy; General Awareness/General Knowledge and Current Affairs.
- Languages: Assamese, Bodo, Bengali and English.

The advertisement describes the written-test subjects broadly. This document does not invent a subject-wise question distribution.

## Import and verification rules

- Treat these as recruitment profiles, not interchangeable track aliases.
- Preserve the difference between historical CWT papers and current recruitment advertisements.
- Keep general Constable AB separate from Commando Constable AB.
- Store provisional answer keys as provisional.
- For a PYQ record, preserve paper code, set, question number, official source URL, and verification status.
- Do not mark an answer officially final until matched against the final key for the same paper and set.
- The question-bank engine must not expose answer keys before submission.
